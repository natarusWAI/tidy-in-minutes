A clean kitchen at night makes the morning easier. This reset takes about ten minutes.

## The routine

1. **Clear the counters** (2 min): put away anything that does not live there.
2. **Load the dishwasher** (3 min): run it, or hand wash what is left.
3. **Wipe surfaces** (3 min): counters, stovetop and sink.
4. **Reset the sink** (1 min): rinse it and leave it empty.
5. **Floor check** (1 min): quick sweep of the high-traffic spot.

## Tip

Set a timer. A visible deadline keeps the routine from stretching.
