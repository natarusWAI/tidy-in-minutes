You do not need a weekend to simplify your wardrobe.

## Steps

1. Take everything out and sort into **keep**, **donate** and **maybe**.
2. Put the *maybe* box away for 30 days. If you do not open it, donate it.
3. Group what you keep by type, then by color.
4. Use matching hangers to make the closet look calm.

The goal is a closet where getting dressed takes less than five minutes.
